# Upscape: free demo

Play it in your browser: https://wolklaw.github.io/upscape-demo/

A fast arcade tower climber: drag or use the arrow keys to run, tap or press Space to jump, bounce off the walls and skip floors to chain combos. This demo is the first 2,000 floors (two zones and seven themed tiers) with all four modes. The full game climbs on to floor 10,000 through 100 tiers.

No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Your scores stay in your browser. Privacy policy: https://wolklaw.github.io/upscape-privacy/
